01Logo

One mark, always the real image

Use the ink logo on light, the white logo on dark. Never type the name instead. Never redraw it.

Ink, on porcelain or white

White, on night or indigo

White on cobalt works too

Ink on the second porcelain

Clear space

Keep the height of the oval mark free on every side. Nothing enters the dashed box: no text, no edge, no photo.

xxxx

Minimum size

300px wide on screen. Smaller than this and the script hairlines break up.

300 px

Four don'ts

Don't stretch or squash

Don't recolour

Don't set on a busy photo

Don't rotate or tilt

06Voice

Like Auntie talking: warm, plain, a little proud

Warm

Calm, long-form, editorial. Open the way Auntie would: “At Auntie Gaik Lean's, …”

SayCome hungry. We have a table.
Don't sayReserve your premium dining experience.

Plain

Short sentences. No exclamation marks.

SayEverything is made by hand, from scratch.
Don't sayArtisanal creations, crafted to perfection!

Proud

State the facts. Let them speak.

SayOne MICHELIN Star, awarded in 2022 and kept every year since.
Don't sayThe best Nyonya in town.

UK English

Spelling, dates and tone.

SayFlavour, colour, Wed to Sun.
Don't sayFlavor, color, Wednesday thru Sunday.
