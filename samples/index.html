Post 01 · People

Meet Auntie

Auntie Gaik Lean started cooking for her family at twelve. She sold homemade kuih by the roadside, cooked in factory canteens and trained chefs in Kuala Lumpur. In 2013 her son Adrian opened this eatery in her name and asked her to come home to run it. Her recipes are still made by hand, from scratch, in our kitchen.
