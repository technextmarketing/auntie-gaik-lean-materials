A month at Auntie's table.

    08

    For the people who write about food.

    A short fact sheet a journalist or hotel concierge can use as it is. Photos are in the library.

    Who

    Auntie Gaik Lean (Beh Gaik Lean), a fourth-generation, self-taught Nyonya cook, and her eldest son Adrian Tan.

    Since

    2013, at 1 Lebuh Bishop (Bishop Street), George Town, Penang.

    Recognition

    One MICHELIN Star in the first MICHELIN Guide for Malaysia, kept every year since: four years running.

    The cooking

    Heirloom family recipes, made by hand and from scratch on site. Pork-free kitchen.

    Try

    Curry Kapitan, Nasi Ulam, Otak-Otak, Gulai Tumis, Assam Prawns, Kuih Pie Tee, Bee Koh Moy.

    Visit

    Wed–Sun, lunch 12pm–2.30pm, dinner 6pm–9.30pm. Closed Monday and Tuesday. Booking ahead is advised.

    Photos: Photo & film library · Brand: Brand guidelines · Menu: Auntie's Tingkat
