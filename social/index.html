02

What works

  1. 1
    A premium, consistent look

    Real photography and one type style keep the grid recognisable.

  2. 2
    Depth of story

    Ingredients, the market, the street and the table. Every post teaches something.

  3. 3
    Proof up front

    The MICHELIN Star, four years running, is pinned and in the bio.

  4. 4
    Real faces

    Auntie, Adrian and the team appear on camera, which is hard to copy.

03

The gaps

  1. 1
    Quiet engagement

    18–44 likes a post on 7.9K followers. Captions rarely ask a question or give a reason to save or share.

  2. 2
    No call to book

    The booking link sits only in the bio. Posts never say how to reserve.

  3. 3
    English only

    No Chinese or Malay lines, and no Xiaohongshu account found, though Penang draws many Chinese-speaking travellers.

  4. 4
    Facebook repeats Instagram

    The same captions, 16–20 reactions a post, and a Featured section from 2023 that could be refreshed.

  5. 5
    A few covers off-style

    Some reel covers use other fonts and colours, such as a yellow title. One cover style would keep the grid whole.

  6. 6
    Little for Google to find

    auntiegaiklean.com shows a "Launching Soon" page, so search and AI answers have little of their own to cite.

04

What we would change

Keep their look and voice. Add reach, conversation and a booking path.

Rhythm

Three a week

One reel, one carousel and one single post, using the same pillars: dish stories, people, the room, Penang and moments.

Booking

A line on every post

Every caption ends with how to book: the online link, call or WhatsApp. Stories carry a link sticker to the booking page.

Reach

Bilingual and paid

Add English plus Chinese lines, start Xiaohongshu, and run Meta ads to Singapore and Kuala Lumpur travellers.

Conversation

Ask, reply, repost

End posts with a question, reply to every comment, and share guests' posts with their permission.

Facebook

Native, not copied

Shorter captions, Events for festive menus, albums for celebrations, and a refreshed Featured section.

System

One template kit

Fixed cover styles for reels, carousels and promos so the grid never breaks. See the brand guidelines.
